How it works

Draw once. Extract from the whole pile.

  1. Import PDFsDrag them in, or pick a whole folder.
  2. Draw the anchorBox the text that identifies the document type, such as a vendor name or form title. That creates a group.
  3. Add fields and tablesMark single values or whole tables. Columns are split out automatically.
  4. Run and reviewMatching PDFs are read from their text layer, or by OCR when they are scanned. Step through the results on the page.
  5. ExportFix anything by typing into the table, then download an .xlsx.
