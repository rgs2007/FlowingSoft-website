FDP (Fodinha)

The Brazilian trick-taking card game, ready to play in your browser against 1 to 5 bots. Bet how many tricks you will win, lose a life for every trick you miss by, and be the last one standing. Nothing to install, in Portuguese, Spanish or English.

How to play

Bet your tricks. Keep your lives.

  1. The vira sets the manilhaThe turned-up card decides which rank becomes the trump. Between manilhas: clubs, hearts, spades, diamonds.
  2. Everyone betsSay how many tricks you will win. The dealer cannot make the total match the number of tricks.
  3. Play the tricksEqual plain cards cancel each other out. Highest remaining card takes the trick.
  4. Lose livesYou lose one life for each trick you are off. The last player with lives wins.
  5. One-card roundsIn the one-card round, your card goes on your forehead: you see everyone's but your own.
