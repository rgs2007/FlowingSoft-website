Brain Tuning Sounds

Binaural beats with optional white, pink or brown noise, generated live in your browser. Pick a research-based setting for sleep, meditation, relaxation or focus, or tune the beat yourself. A plain-language guide explains each frequency band and how strong the evidence really is.

Product lab result

Popular wellness claims, sorted against the research.

Binaural beat apps often promise a lot. We read the studies first, built the presets around the frequencies researchers actually tested, and wrote down what the evidence supports and where it is thin. Checking claims before trusting a tool is the same habit we bring to AI work with clients.

Research-based presets

Delta for sleep, theta for meditation, alpha for relaxing, beta for focus and 40 Hz gamma, each with its source studies.

Honest about evidence

Every frequency band is labeled with how strong the research is, from some support to preliminary.

Private and free

All sound is generated on your device. No account, no download, nothing recorded or uploaded.

How to use it

Headphones on. Volume low.

  1. Put on headphonesEach ear gets a slightly different tone. Your brain hears the difference as a soft pulse, which speakers cannot produce.
  2. Pick a common settingDeep sleep, meditation, relax, focus or 40 Hz gamma. Each one sets the beat, tone, noise and timer.
  3. Adjust if you wantChange the beat frequency, base tone, noise color, balance and stop timer.
  4. Listen for a set timeSessions fade out gently at the end. Do not use it while driving.
